Summer Soccer Tournament 2026

Teams Games Rules & Info Check-In

Tournament Format

  • 9 teams participate.
  • Each team plays 6 games
  • Teams will switch between the East and West fields throughout the tournament. This helps keep the games moving and cuts down on long waits and byes.
  • After League Stage, all 9 teams are combined into one overall standings table.
  • Top 4 advance to Semi-Finals: 1st vs 4th and 2nd vs 3rd.
  • Semi-Finals winners advance to the Final.
  • The East Field will be available for a third-place game if the two teams decide to play.
  • Tied Semi-Finals and Final go directly to penalty kicks. If the scores remain tied after five shots, the shootout goes to sudden death.
  • Cash prize for the winning team

Game Format

  • Try to wear a shirt that matches your team color as closely as possible.
  • League stage games: 20 minutes.
  • Semi-Finals: 30 minutes.
  • Final: 30 minutes.
  • Third place: 30 minutes.
  • All games are played straight through with no halftime.
  • Please arrive 15 minutes early to find your teammates and be ready at your assigned field before kickoff.

Standings

  • Win = 3 points
  • Draw = 1 point
  • Loss = 0 points

Tiebreakers:

  1. Points
  2. Goal differential
  3. Goals scored
  4. Head-to-head
  5. Additional tiebreaker if needed
